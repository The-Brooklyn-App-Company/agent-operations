# Operator handoff

- **From / to:** `<role>` -> `<role>`
- **Repository and principal:** `<repo>` / `<author or reviewer>`
- **Date:** `<YYYY-MM-DD>`

## Observed

Record facts with their source and freshness, such as a SHA, link or timestamp.

-

## Proposed

Describe each action and its effect. Label it existing or proposed new.

-

## Approved

Record who approved what, when, and the scope of the approval.

-

## Executed

Record what was done, with the resulting SHA, link or ID. Include anything skipped and the reason.

-

## Open

Compare the executed step with the full authorized outcome. For remaining work, name the existing owner and next action; distinguish ready authorized work from a real dependency or parked scope. For a wait, name the verified existing result path or checkpoint. Link the owning record rather than creating a second task list.
